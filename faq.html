Filling Out the Form

Each skill is scored on a scale of 0 to 4:
  • 0Not at All — Student did not perform the skill.
  • 1Direct Prompting — Required hands-on or step-by-step guidance.
  • 2Indirect Prompting — Needed a verbal reminder or gesture.
  • 3Independent — Performed the skill without any prompting.
  • 4Helping/Teaching — Performed the skill and helped or modeled it for others.
Behavioral Skills are the 16 core work-readiness skills that apply at every site — things like punctuality, communication, and attitude. You score these every time.

Job Tasks are specific to the job site — for example, a grocery site might have tasks like "Facing shelves" or "Operating register." These only appear if tasks have been set up for that site by your coordinator.
N/A means the task didn't come up that day — it wasn't applicable. For example, if a student's job task includes "Operating the baler" but the baler wasn't needed that shift, select N/A. This keeps it out of their averages rather than counting as a zero. Job tasks default to N/A, so you only need to change it if the task actually happened.
Students are managed by your program coordinator in the Admin panel. If a student is missing, contact your coordinator and ask them to add the student to the system. You won't be able to submit an entry until they appear in the list.
Notes are optional but encouraged. Use them to record anything specific that happened — a particularly strong or challenging moment, a behavior that context helps explain, or anything that a score alone doesn't capture. Notes show up on the student's evaluation history in reports.

Submitting & Troubleshooting

After tapping Submit, the button will turn green and show a checkmark with "Saved!" The form will then reset automatically so you're ready for the next entry. If the button stays blue or shows an error, the save may have failed — check your internet connection and try again.
If you saw the green "Saved!" confirmation, the entry was recorded even if the reset seemed slow. If you're unsure, contact your coordinator — they can check the data directly in the spreadsheet to confirm whether it came through.
Try these steps in order:
  1. Check that your phone has an internet connection (WiFi or cellular).
  2. Close the browser tab and reopen the link or re-scan the QR code.
  3. Try a different browser (Chrome or Safari work best).
  4. If none of those work, let your coordinator know — the system may need attention on their end.
