fact_check Scoring Rubric Reference

Use this same scale for behavioral skills and scored job tasks.

0 · Not at All
Student did not perform the skill.
1 · Direct Prompting
Required direct or step-by-step prompting.
2 · Indirect Prompting
Needed a reminder, cue, or gesture.
3 · Independent
Performed without prompting.
4 · Helping / Teaching
Performed independently and helped or modeled for others.

Job tasks may be marked N/A when the task did not occur during the shift.
